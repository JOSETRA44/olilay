UNICALI was created to organize the academic information a student handles every term: grades, averages, schedules, materials and the experience of fellow students.

Its design principle is privacy: academic records live on the student's device, not on someone else's server. Community features, such as reviews or the social campus, are built separately for those who choose to take part.
