## What data we collect

This site only collects personal data when you write to us through the contact form: name, email, organization (optional), topic and the content of your message.

We do not use tracking cookies, advertising or third-party analytics. Your light or dark theme preference is stored only in your browser (local storage) and is never sent to any server.

## What we use it for

We use your data solely to reply to your inquiry and, where appropriate, to continue the conversation about the project or information you requested. We do not use it for unsolicited marketing, and we do not share it with third parties for their own purposes.

## Legal basis

We process your data on the basis of your consent, which you give by ticking the corresponding box before submitting the form.

## Providers involved

To operate the site we use providers that may process data on our behalf:

- **Cloudflare**, for hosting and delivering the website.
- **Resend**, for delivering form messages by email.

These providers may process data outside your country of residence, under their own contractual data protection safeguards.

## How long we keep it

We keep messages for as long as needed to handle your inquiry and any relationship arising from it. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

## Your rights

You may request access to, correction or deletion of your data, object to its processing or withdraw your consent by writing to [ventas@olilay.com](mailto:ventas@olilay.com).

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page with its update date.
